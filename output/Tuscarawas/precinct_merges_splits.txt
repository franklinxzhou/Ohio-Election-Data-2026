Method and limitations
  The 2024 geometry serves as a proxy for the 2026 boundaries. Join by
  numeric precinct code except Warwick. In the 2026 canvass and November
  sample ballots, 077 is West and 078 is East. The 2024 geometry and the
  county precinct list use the reverse code order. The crosswalk follows
  the East and West names for those two precincts. The ballot and canvass
  spellings of East differ. The CSV keeps the canvass label.
  The county precinct list distinguishes Dennison Village 1 (057) and 2
  (058), both of which appear as Dennison Vlg in the canvass.
  In EPSG:5070, retain intersections covering at least 2% of a 2024
  precinct or 95% of a 2020 VTD. Multiple accepted VTDs are marked
  boundary_overlap. A sole overlap covering under 95% of the old VTD is
  marked split_precinct. Percentages are polygon area shares. Graph IDs
  include all nodes carrying each retained VTD name. combined_node
  identifies consolidation in the graph. Each precinct keeps its votes.
