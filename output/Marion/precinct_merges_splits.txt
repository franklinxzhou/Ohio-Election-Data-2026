Method
  The two polygon layers were intersected in EPSG:5070. The percentages
  below are shares of mapped 2024 precinct area. Links retain each 2020
  precinct covering at least 2% of that area, or whose historical area
  is at least 95% contained. Green Camp Village is fully contained in
  current Green Camp Township and accounts for 1.38% of its mapped area.
  The 2026 canvass reports the village levy under that township (p. 125).
  A merged_precinct covers at least 95% of each listed predecessor.
  Other multi-precinct links
  are marked boundary_overlap. Groups repeated across graph nodes keep
  all applicable node IDs. Votes stay on their 2026 canvass rows.
