Method
  Canvass codes join to county map BOEPREID codes. The export uses
  CC for CAN.C, NC for N.CAN and MLAKE for MLAKA. The county map has
  276 features. Parts sharing a code are dissolved to 274 precincts.
  The county layer includes edits dated after election day. The code
  roster matches the export, while exact boundary vintage is uncertain.
  The source is a CSV and the source_pages field is blank.
  Intersect the county and 2020 VTD polygons in EPSG:5070. Retain a
  predecessor if it covers at least 2% of the current polygon or the
  current polygon covers at least 95% of the predecessor. Small overlaps
  may reflect boundary offsets. When the graph repeats a historic
  membership across district-split nodes, retain all candidate IDs.
  Each canvass row retains its own votes without allocation.

Examples from the overlay
  Alliance 1-B covers virtually all of former 1-B and 1-D.
  Canton 8-B draws 87.81% of its area from former Canton 8-C and
  11.92% from former Plain Township 6. Current Canton 8-E draws
  97.38% of its area from former Canton 8-F.
  Massillon 6-B draws roughly equal areas from former 6-A and 6-B.
  North Canton 3-B combines land from former 3-B and 2-C, both in
  graph node 6816.
