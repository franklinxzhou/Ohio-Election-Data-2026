The CSV preserves the report's precinct names. Its headings supply
no separate precinct codes. Name matching omits City, Twp, and
Village where the graph does. Windham township and village labels
retain their qualifiers so the two sets of precincts stay distinct.

Four graph nodes have two separate canvass rows each
  6175 | Franklin Twp E, Kent City 4B
  6181 | Hiram Twp B, Hiram Village
  6223 | Ravenna City 2C, Ravenna Twp G
  6232 | Ravenna Twp D, Ravenna Twp E

The report lists 18 current Kent precincts. Kent 3D (6193),
6C (6200), and 6D (6201) were eliminated after ward boundary changes.
The county GIS layer still shows the old labels. The CSV links the
surviving Kent labels by name. The successor boundaries of the three
retired precincts and the boundaries of surviving labels remain
unverified. Graph node 6264, Voting Districts Not Defined, also has
no corresponding report row.
