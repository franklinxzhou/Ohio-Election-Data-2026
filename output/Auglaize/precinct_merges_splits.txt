Precinct boundary changes relative to the graph
  Union W (0310, pp. 113-114) and Union E (0315, pp. 117-118) each lie
  more than 99.8% within the 2020 UNION voting district (GEOID 39011006ABL,
  graph node 314). Both rows link to node 314 as split_precinct. The 2020
  report lists Union, and the 2024 report lists Union W and Union E.
  https://www.boe.ohio.gov/auglaize/c/elecres/20201103precinct.pdf#page=109
  https://www.boe.ohio.gov/auglaize/c/elecres/20241105precinct.pdf#page=121
  https://www.boe.ohio.gov/auglaize/c/elecres/20241105precinct.pdf#page=125

  New Bremen S (0395, pp. 153-154) lies about 99.8% within the 2020
  NEW BREMEN E district (GEOID 39011006AAV, graph node 303). New Bremen S
  links to node 303 as split_precinct. New Bremen E (0390, pp. 149-150)
  also links to node 303 by name.

  St. Marys S (0280, pp. 101-102) overlaps the 2020 ST. MARYS E district
  by about 71.8% (GEOID 39011006ABH, graph node 305) and ST. MARYS W by
  about 28.2% (GEOID 39011006ABJ, graph node 310). Its row links to both
  as boundary_overlap.

  Percentages estimate the share of each current precinct's area by
  overlaying the county GIS polygons with the 2020 Census polygons.
  Intersections below 0.3% were treated as boundary discrepancies.
  The CSV keeps one set of reported vote totals per current precinct.
