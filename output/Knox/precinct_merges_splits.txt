Splits and other boundary changes
  Milford A and Milford B cover 44.04% and 55.76% of old MILFORD
  (4210), respectively. Pleasant A and Pleasant B cover 35.51% and
  63.10% of old PLEASANT (part of 4216).
  Fredericktown A covers 93.81% of old FREDERICKTOWN A and 98.98%
  of old FREDERICKTOWN C. It also overlaps old WAYNE (14.82% of
  current Fredericktown A's area).
  Clinton A is mostly former CLINTON C. Clinton B includes most of
  former CLINTON A and CLINTON B. The rows below show the mapped
  shares for these and the other overlaps.

Graph nodes with multiple 2020 precinct names
  4186 and 4187: CLINTON A, CLINTON B and MOUNT VERNON 1-B. The

    Both graph IDs are retained for overlaps with that group.
  4188: CLINTON C and MORRIS B.

Method
  County map parts were grouped by precinct and intersected with Census
  precinct polygons in EPSG:5070. Map CLAY matches canvass CLAY-MART and
  Census CLAY-MARTINSBURG. Mount Vernon's ward labels omit the city name
  in the canvass and county map.
  Each percentage below is the mapped share of the current precinct.
  Graph links include all historical precincts covering at least 2% of
  that area. Small overlaps may reflect differences in the map sources.
  Votes remain on their original canvass rows. Area shares record geometry.
