Historical boundary changes
  The county layer was joined to the canvass by name. GIS abbreviations,
  spacing and zero padding differ, and GIS codes can be reused. For each
  county polygon the crosswalk includes 2020 voting districts covering
  at least 2% of its mapped area. Shares are approximate, measured on a
  raster in the common GIS projection (EPSG:3857, about 1,700 pixels along
  each current precinct's longest side). Small overlaps are omitted.
  Graph IDs are the union of the retained districts' ORIG_NAME matches.
  Area percentages describe geography. Votes remain in their own precinct
  rows in full and are not allocated across graph nodes.

  CITY 21, CARL 5, and MFLD 9 each occupy a portion of one historical
  district (CITY 11, CARL 1, and MFLD 1, respectively). CITY 27 tracks
  historical CITY 14 with approximately the same footprint.
  The first published returns found for CARL 4 and CARL 5 are from the
  May 2026 primary. Both were absent in the November 2025 results.
