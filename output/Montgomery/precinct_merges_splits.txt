Ballot styles and geography
  A terminal space and number in a canvass label identifies a ballot
  style. The 468 reported rows are summed to 381 base precincts.
  GIS parts sharing VLABEL are dissolved. Zero-padding in ward numbers
  is removed from the GIS label to match the canvass code. The county
  layer contains all canvass base labels. Later changes to the geometry
  may be missing.

  Links come from polygon intersections in EPSG:5070. Retain a
  predecessor if it covers at least 2% of current area or the current
  precinct covers at least 95% of its area. Percentages are shares of
  current and former polygon area. Small overlaps may be geometry offsets.
  Multiple retained predecessors are marked boundary_overlap. A renamed
  precinct carved from a single predecessor is marked split_precinct.
  When historical graph membership repeats across districts, all IDs
  for that membership are retained as candidates. No votes are allocated
  across IDs here. Combined graph members preserve separate canvass rows.

  Each crosswalk row gives canvass label, GIS name, match status, graph
  node IDs, and historical overlaps.

Examples of boundary changes
  Current Dayton 19-B covers 99.85% of former Dayton 19-C. Current
  Dayton 19-C draws 99.80% of its area from former Dayton 19-D.
  Current Riverside N, O and P each draw from two former precincts.
  Washington Township AA, BB and CC each draw from three.
