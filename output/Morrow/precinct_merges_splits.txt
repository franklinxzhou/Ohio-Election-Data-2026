Graph node 5908 includes Cardington Township and Cardington Village
East. The canvass reports Cardington Twp and Cardington Vlg E separately.
Both retain their own vote rows. Cardington Village West is node 5909.

The other 31 precinct names link one-to-one to graph nodes. Name
matching expands Vlg to Village and, for Bennington, Chester, and
Congress townships, E and W to East and West. The CSV preserves
the canvass spellings. The source prints precinct names only.

The name-based crosswalk identifies no additional mergers or splits.
Boundary continuity was not assessed.
