Mount Orab Village West
  0081 Mount Orab Village West (pp. 22-23) links to node 395 as
  split_precinct. Brown County Engineer's May 2021 voting-precinct
  shapefile identifies code 81 as MT ORAB STERLING. The county's November
  2021 canvass uses the Mount Orab Village West name for the same code.
  The shapefile boundary overlaps historical MT ORAB NORTH (2020 Census
  GEOID 39015008ACG, node 395) by about 99.5% of its area. Under 1%
  overlaps historical Sterling Twp South, mostly in a narrow strip along
  the precinct boundary. The graph link uses the dominant overlap.
  Current 0070 Mt Orab North (pp. 16-17) also links to node 395 by name.
  Both vote rows retain their separately reported totals.
