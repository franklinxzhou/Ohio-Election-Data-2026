Method and limitations
  Match abbreviated canvass names to the 2024 geography and graph
  through the county roster. For precincts with documented 2025 or 2026
  changes, use the county notices and maps to identify contributing 2020
  precincts. These map-based links are qualitative, with no 2026 overlap
  percentages. Other rows show the 2024 and 2020 area shares, retaining
  overlaps of at least 2% of 2024 area or 95% of 2020 area. The historic
  comparison does not establish the exact boundary of the reporting unit
  in November 2026. If multiple qualifying 2020 precincts share a graph
  node, the CSV lists that ID once. Each precinct retains its vote totals.
