Crosswalk method
  Current polygons were joined to the official labels by GIS NAME
  and intersected with historical districts in equal-area EPSG:5070.
  A historical district is retained when it covers at least 2% of
  a current precinct. Each share describes current precinct area.
  The same-name match applies when it is the sole retained historical
  district, allowing the graph's city abbreviations: COLS, CANAL WIN,
  DUB, GAH, HILL, REYNS, UA, and WORTH.
  A different single predecessor uses renamed when the footprints
  overlap by at least 98% in both directions, or split_precinct when
  the old district covers at least 98% of the current precinct and
  the current precinct covers less than 98% of the old district.
  Other changed links use boundary_overlap. The CSV keeps each precinct's
  full vote counts on its own row.
