Findings
  All 50 canvass precincts match individual 2020 precinct names after
  accounting for abbreviations and slash spacing. The 2020–2024 polygon
  comparison supports those links. The minimum same-name area shares
  are 99.94% of 2024 area and 99.91% of 2020 area, rounded to two decimals.
  These polygons cover 2020 and 2024. The 2026 links use canvass names.
  No precinct merges or splits were identified in these sources.
