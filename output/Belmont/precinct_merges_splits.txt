Flushing Township precinct consolidation
  Current 047 FLUSHING TWP (2026 canvass p. 4) links to former 046
  FLUSHING TWP 1 (node 337) and former 047 FLUSHING TWP 2 (node 338).
  The November 2024 canvass lists both former precincts. The November
  2025 turnout table lists a single township precinct labeled 047
  FLUSHING TWP 1. The May 2026 turnout table calls it 047 FLUSHING TWP.
  The crosswalk infers the consolidation from these canvasses. The
  CSV keeps the current township's vote totals on one row.
