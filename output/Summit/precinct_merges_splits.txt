Method and limitations
  The 2026 canvass names join to the published 2024 precinct shapes.
  Graph matches use 2024 boundaries as a proxy for 2026. The county maps
  for GREEN 1-A, 1-B and 1-C record July 7, 2026 revisions. Their CSV graph
  IDs are blank pending revised geometry. Their former overlaps are listed
  below as 2024 evidence. Other post-2024 edits are possible.
  In EPSG:5070, an intersection is retained if it covers at least 2% of
  the 2024 precinct or at least 95% of the 2020 VTD. Percentages are land
  and water polygon area shares. Multiple 2020 overlaps are listed as
  boundary_overlap. A single accepted overlap covering under 95% of the
  former VTD is listed as split_precinct. Graph IDs include all nodes
  carrying each retained VTD name. The CSV preserves precinct vote totals.
