Method and changes
  The two polygon layers were intersected in EPSG:5070. Links retain
  predecessors covering at least 2% of the newer polygon or at least
  95% of the predecessor. Shares below are of newer mapped area.
  Eleven name variants differ in slash spacing. Small overlaps may
  reflect mapping differences. Existing graph groups retain their shared
  IDs and each canvass precinct keeps its own votes.
  Former Huber Heights B is split among the four current Huber Heights
  precincts. Current C also includes land from Bethel South Central.
  The mapped B and D precincts fall entirely inside former B and account
  for 34.01% and 28.55% of former B's area, respectively.
