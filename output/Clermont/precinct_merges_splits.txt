Historical boundary changes
  The crosswalk retains historical districts covering at least 2% of a
  current precinct's area. Shares are GIS raster estimates in EPSG:3857
  at about 1,700 pixels along each current precinct's longest side.
  Shares within 0.05 percentage points of the cutoff were refined at
  6,800 pixels. The Union Township X to historical Union Township G overlap
  was 1.95% after refinement, so its graph link uses Union Township X.
  Historical names map to all graph IDs listing that ORIG_NAME.
  Small overlaps are omitted. Rows retain their original vote counts.

  Loveland City C is predominantly within historical Loveland City B.
  Batavia Township P is predominantly within historical Batavia TWP C.
  Both are recorded as split_precinct. The shares describe the current
  footprints and do not establish how or when the boundaries were set.
