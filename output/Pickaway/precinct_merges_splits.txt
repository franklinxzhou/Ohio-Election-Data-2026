Boundary method
  The county layer excludes the City of Circleville, so the city layer
  supplies precincts 1–9. Polygon parts with the same name are dissolved.
  Keep overlaps covering at least 2% of the current area or 95% of the
  former area, plus predecessors explicitly named in the county notice.
  These are polygon area shares. Small changes may reflect map offsets.
  The workbook's precinct labels and unallocated vote counts are preserved.
  For XLSX provenance, source_pages lists workbook sheet names.

Documented changes
  Former Ashville-North was divided into Northeast and Northwest along
  Long Street. Both rows link to node 6089. The Northwest polygon
  also overlaps former Harrison Township (6088).
  Former Commercial Point East is now Central. Central also took Walker
  Point from Scioto South. The new Commercial Point East contains Foxfire,
  formerly in Scioto North. Commercial Point West took Chestnut Commons
  from Scioto North. The graph already groups former Commercial Point East,
  Commercial Point West, and Scioto North in 6101. Central also links to
  Scioto South's 6116.

Changes inferred from the polygon overlay
  Former South Bloomfield (6117) is divided among South Bloomfield
  North, South, and West. West also overlaps former Harrison Township
  (6088), which accounts for 21.70% of its current polygon.
