Countywide reconciliation
  The canvass lists one ballot in a separate Cumulative reporting
  group. That group has no precinct code and is excluded from the
  precinct CSV. Use the precinct-only County - Total before the group
  for reconciliation. The later County - Total includes the group.
  The CSV uses published precinct Total rows and leaves withheld
  ballot-mode values unused.
