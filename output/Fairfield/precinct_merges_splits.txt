Crosswalk method
  Shapefile ALLCAPS codes were matched to the canvass. Lancaster
  ward codes receive LAN- and Lithopolis split codes are grouped
  by the LIV-A or LIV-B prefix. All pieces are unioned by code.
  Intersections use equal-area EPSG:5070. A historical district
  is retained when it covers at least 2% of a current precinct.
  The same-name match applies when it is the sole retained historical
  district, after expanding the report's precinct abbreviations.
  A changed single predecessor is recorded as renamed when both
  footprints overlap by at least 98%, or split_precinct when the
  old district covers at least 98% of the current precinct and
  the current precinct covers less than 98% of the old district.
  The remaining changes use boundary_overlap. Votes remain on their
  original precinct rows.
