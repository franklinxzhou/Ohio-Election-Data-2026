  was summed for the statewide contests. The CSV includes pages for

Changes and shared graph nodes
  All 163 named historical precinct labels also appear in the county
  GIS. Some precincts retain their names after their boundaries change.
  Painesville City 4A corresponds mostly to the former 4D, including
  79.16% of old 4D. Painesville City 4D covers 98.81% of former 4A.
  Painesville City 3C contains 41.70% of the former 4C area.
  Concord Twp M contains 28.45% of the former Concord Twp I area.
  4254 groups FAIRPORT HBR BB, GRAND RIVER AA, PAINESVILLE CITY 1A,
    PAINESVILLE CITY 4B, and PAINESVILLE TWP J.
  4318 groups PAINESVILLE CITY 2B and PAINESVILLE TWP D.
  4337 groups PERRY TWP C and PERRY VILLAGE AA.
  4283 and 4284 both represent MENTOR CITY 1I, in House districts 23
    and 57. Both IDs are retained wherever the historic precinct links.
  4340 and 4341 are Census areas labeled VOTING DISTRICTS NOT DEFINED.
    They do not correspond to a canvass precinct and are excluded.

Method
  The two map layers were intersected in EPSG:5070. Percentages below
  refer to the current precinct's mapped area. The crosswalk retains
  named historic precincts covering at least 2% of that area. Other
  precincts link by name. Small overlaps can reflect map differences.
  Votes remain on the 163 precinct rows and are never apportioned
  across graph nodes.
