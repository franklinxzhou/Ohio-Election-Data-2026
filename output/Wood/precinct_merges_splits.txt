Method and limitations
  The 2024 boundaries serve as a proxy for 2026. Canvass names join to
  the 2024 features after reconciling township and village abbreviations.
  Retain intersections covering at least 2% of the 2024 precinct or
  95% of the 2020 VTD, measured in EPSG:5070. Multiple qualifying old
  precincts are marked boundary_overlap. A single overlap covering less
  than 95% of the old precinct is marked split_precinct. The crosswalk
  lists all graph IDs associated with each qualifying precinct name.
  For graph pairs 7861/7862 and 7864/7865, the Ohio House tables in
  Raw/Wood.pdf, pp. 59–64, identify which populated graph nodes serve
  each precinct. Only Perrysburg Twp Ft Meigs appears in both House
  districts within those two groups. The Perrysburg A, B and U rows
  retain the zero-2020-population HD44 geographic nodes, alongside the
  populated HD75 nodes. Their House votes appear in HD75 alone.
  The vote CSV retains precinct totals.
