Crosswalk method
  Current polygons were joined with the five-digit report key and
  intersected with Census districts in equal-area EPSG:5070.
  Historical districts covering at least 2% of a current precinct
  are retained. Each share is a percentage of current precinct area.
  The same-name match applies when it is the sole retained historical
  district, allowing the canvass's CITY qualifier and matching the
  distinct Delaware A/B labels to Delaware TWP A/B.
  A different single predecessor is recorded as renamed when both
  footprints overlap by at least 98%, or split_precinct when the
  historical district covers at least 98% of the current precinct
  and the current precinct covers less than 98% of the historical
  one. The other changed links use boundary_overlap.
  Each current precinct keeps a separate row with its full vote counts.
