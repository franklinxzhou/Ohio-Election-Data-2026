Method
  Intersect both layers in EPSG:5070. Retain historical precincts
  covering at least 2% of current area, or at least 95% of the former
  precinct. Printed shares refer to polygon area. Small overlaps
  may reflect offsets between the source layers. Where the only
  retained predecessor has the same precinct code, use that code's
  graph link. Each current precinct retains its own votes.
