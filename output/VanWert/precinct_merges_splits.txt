Canvass reporting rows
  The official scan labels 32 precincts as reporting and lists 39 vote
  rows. The CSV aggregates the following rows to precinct totals:
    Jennings = Jennings 1, Jennings 2, Jennings 3, Jennings 4
    Pleasant North = Pleasant North 1, Pleasant North 2
    Union = Union 1, Union 2
    Washington West = Washington West 1, Washington West 2
    York = York 1 & 3, York 2
  The scanned report has no separate precinct code field.

Graph crosswalk
  Published 2024 geometry is a proxy for the 2026 precinct boundaries.
  Current shapes join to precinct names. The intersection calculation
  uses EPSG:5070. An overlap qualifies if its area is at least 2% of
  the 2024 precinct or at least 95% of the 2020 voting district.
  A boundary_overlap row retains multiple historic precincts. Graph
  IDs cover every qualifying 2020 name. A combined_node row links to a
  graph node with multiple member precincts. Votes remain on their
  canvass precinct rows for later recasting.
