Method and limitations
  The April 2025 map serves as a proxy for November 2026 boundaries.
  The GIS overlay retains intersections of at least 2% of the mapped
  precinct or at least 95% of the old precinct. Multiple retained 2020
  districts are marked boundary_overlap. One retained district covering
  less than 95% of its 2020 area is marked split_precinct. A split_node
  result lists multiple graph nodes associated with one old precinct.
  A combined_node lists a graph node with multiple old member precincts.
  All percentages are EPSG:5070 polygon area shares. The CSV retains
  the full canvass totals for each 2026 precinct.
