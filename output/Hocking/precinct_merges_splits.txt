Merged precinct
  19 WARD links to CARBON HILL (4020) and MURRAY CITY (4033).
  The county map covers 99.33% of the old CARBON HILL area and 99.50%
  of the old MURRAY CITY area. The two precincts were reported separately
  in the 2019 official results (p. 38). The 2023 official results list
  the Murray City village levy under WARD (p. 68).
  https://www.boe.ohio.gov/hocking/c/elecres/20191105precinct.pdf
  https://www.boe.ohio.gov/hocking/c/elecres/20231107precinct.pdf
