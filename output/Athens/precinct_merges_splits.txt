Whole-precinct mergers approved for 2026
  0004 Athens Ward 1, Precinct 4 (pp. 16-18) combines former Athens 1-4
  (node 250) and Athens 1-5 (251).
  0005 Athens Ward 2, Precinct 1 (pp. 21-23) combines former Athens 2-1
  (252) and Athens 2-5 (256).
  0006 Athens Ward 2, Precinct 2 (pp. 26-28) combines former Athens 2-2
  (253) and Athens 2-3 (254).
  0008 Athens Ward 3, Precinct 1 (pp. 36-38) combines former Athens 3-1
  (257) and Athens 3-3 (259).
  0034 Dover East (pp. 166-168) combines former Millfield (277) and
  Sugar Creek (284).

Former Athens East split for 2026
  0027 Athens South (pp. 131-133) absorbed the Richland Fire District
  portion of Athens East. 0028 Plains 1 (pp. 136-138) absorbed the Plains
  Fire District portion. Former Athens East, Athens South, and Plains 1
  all belong to the historical graph group represented by nodes 247 and 248.
