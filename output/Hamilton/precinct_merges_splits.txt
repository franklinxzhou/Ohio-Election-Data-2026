Crosswalk method
  GIS PRECINCT codes join to the workbook's four-digit identifiers.
  Polygon intersections use equal-area EPSG:5070. A historical
  district is retained when it covers at least 2% of a current
  precinct's mapped area. Shares describe current area.
  If the sole retained district has the expected name, the crosswalk
  uses that name after expanding abbreviations in the extractor.
  The workbook's HARR 76xx codes are Harrison Township and 34xx
  codes are Harrison City. A different single predecessor is renamed
  when both footprints overlap by at least 98%. It is split_precinct
  when the old district covers at least 98% of the current precinct
  and the current precinct covers less than 98% of the old district.
  The other changes use boundary_overlap. Votes stay on their
  original precinct rows.
