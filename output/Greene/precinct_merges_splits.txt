Crosswalk method
  The GIS PRECINCT field joins to the workbook code with leading zeroes
  restored. Precinct polygons are intersected with historical districts
  in equal-area EPSG:5070. Each retained historical district covers
  at least 2% of the current precinct. Shares describe current area.
  The same-code match applies when it is the sole retained historical
  district. Graph labels store codes last and abbreviate some names.
  The four Census SUGARCREEK labels with a leading PRECINCT use the
  corresponding graph names without that prefix.

  A different single predecessor is renamed when the footprints
  overlap by at least 98% in both directions. It is split_precinct
  when the historical district covers at least 98% of the current
  precinct and the current precinct covers less than 98% of the
  historical one. Other changed links use boundary_overlap. Each
  precinct retains its full vote counts on its own row.
