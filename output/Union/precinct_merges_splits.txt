Method
  The county GIS precinct names join to the election report names.
  The GIS code field differs from the canvass code sequence and repeats
  three codes across six Jerome precincts. CSV codes come from the canvass.
  Retain polygon intersections covering at least 2% of the current
  precinct or at least 95% of the 2020 VTD. Multiple qualifying VTDs
  are marked boundary_overlap. A sole overlap covering less than 95%
  of the 2020 VTD is marked split_precinct. Graph IDs include all nodes
  carrying the qualifying VTD names. Combined_node refers to a graph
  node with multiple source precincts. All percentages are area shares.
  Each precinct's vote totals are kept together.
