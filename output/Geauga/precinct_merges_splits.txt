Bainbridge Township split
  BAINBRIDGE TOWNSHIP J (pp. 6, 11, 16) links to 3094, 3096, and
  3098 as boundary_overlap. These are historical 007 BAINBRIDGE TWP B,
  009 BAINBRIDGE TWP D, and 011 BAINBRIDGE TWP F. Current B, D, and F
  retain their individual graph links as split_precinct.
  The January 8, 2026 board minutes, PDF p. 2, approve precinct J.
  A county elections employee identified B, D, and F as the
  contributing precincts in the February 12 report.
  https://www.boe.ohio.gov/geauga/c/pdfs/minutes/2026/01082026MeetingMinutes.pdf
  https://www.geaugamapleleaf.com/news/chardon-bainbridge-voting-precincts-adjusted/

Chardon City changes
  CHARDON CITY C (pp. 6, 12, 17) links to 3107 and 3108 as
  boundary_overlap. CHARDON CITY D (pp. 7, 12, 17) links to 3108 as
  split_precinct. The January 8 board minutes, PDF p. 2, approve
  moving part of D into C.
  Node 3107 also links to CHARDON CITY B (pp. 6, 12, 17), the other
  member of its historical combined group.
  3107 ORIG_NAME = 020 CHARDON CITY B_GEAUGA,021 CHARDON CITY C_GEAUGA
  3108 ORIG_NAME = 022 CHARDON CITY D_GEAUGA
  https://www.boe.ohio.gov/geauga/c/pdfs/minutes/2026/01082026MeetingMinutes.pdf

Aquilla Village label change
  CLARIDON TOWNSHIP D (pp. 8, 13, 18) links to node 3122 as renamed.
  The March 3, 2025 board minutes, PDF p. 5, designate the former
  Aquilla Village precinct as Claridon Township Precinct D.
  ORIG_NAME = 036 AQUILLA VILLAGE_GEAUGA
