Crosswalk
  The map has a polygon for each of the 210 canvass names. Its
  Brighton and Brownhelm polygon labels are transposed. The township
  maps and Census locations identify Brighton inland and Brownhelm
  to the north. The script corrects the two geometry labels.
  https://www.voteloraincountyohio.gov/_files/ugd/2568d0_8bb2ab3c93d44ce68f7d4d4f9d33448d.pdf
  https://www.voteloraincountyohio.gov/_files/ugd/2568d0_c1e13431e62b4a3ca99097504bd109f2.pdf
  https://www2.census.gov/geo/pvs/bas/bas24maps/st39_oh/cou/c39093_lorain/BAS24C23909300000.pdf
  SHEF LAKE matches SHEF. LAKE. The Census names for Camden and
  Sheffield Township have a PRECINCT prefix omitted by the graph.
  Old district 4753 (VOTING DISTRICTS NOT DEFINED) is excluded.
  Both current Henrietta rows link to old HENRIETTA TWP. North
  Ridgeville 2-E and 2-F come from former 1-D. Wellington Village
  East and West both overlap former Wellington Village South.
  Mixed overlaps in LaGrange, Avon, Eaton and other areas are listed
  below. The mapped shares describe geography, with small overlaps
  potentially reflecting differences between sources.

Method
  The 2026 and 2020 polygons were intersected in EPSG:5070. The
  records below include every named 2020 precinct covering at least
  2% of the current precinct's mapped area where the retained names
  differ from a one-to-one name match. A sole different predecessor
  covering at least 98% of the current polygon is marked renamed if
  the overlap also covers 98% of the predecessor, or split_precinct
  otherwise. Votes stay on their current canvass rows.
