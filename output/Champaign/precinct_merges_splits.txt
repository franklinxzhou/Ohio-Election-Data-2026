Goshen and Wayne splits
  0012 GOSHEN NORTH and 0013 GOSHEN SOUTH link to node 696
  (GOSHEN PRECINCT_CHAMPAIGN). 0030 WAYNE NORTH and 0031 WAYNE SOUTH
  link to node 716 (WAYNE PRECINCT_CHAMPAIGN).
  The 2024 official canvass lists unsplit GOSHEN and WAYNE on p. 23.
  The May 2026 primary canvass lists the Goshen pair on PDF p. 22 and
  a single 0029 WAYNE on PDF p. 128. The November 2026 official canvass
  lists the Goshen pair together on its Goshen Township levy on p. 27
  and the Wayne pair together on its Wayne Township levy on p. 30.

Mad River C
  The May 2026 official primary canvass lists 0019 MAD RIVER A and
  0020 MAD RIVER B, followed by North Lewisburg (PDF p. 15). The November
  official canvass adds 0021 MAD RIVER C (p. 1). The county's map for
  code 0021 still depicts North Lewisburg. Nodes 701 (historical MAD
  RIVER A) and 702 (historical MAD RIVER B) are possible predecessors.
  With the source boundaries unresolved, the CSV leaves MAD RIVER C
  unmatched. Links for MAD RIVER A and MAD RIVER B follow their names.
