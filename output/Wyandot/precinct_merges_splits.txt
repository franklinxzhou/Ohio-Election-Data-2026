Findings
  All 23 2026 canvass names match distinct 2020 precinct names after
  expanding Upper to Upper Sandusky, adding Village for Carey and
  Nevada, and adding TWP to the bare township names. All 17 graph
  nodes are represented.
  No precinct merges or splits identified. The corresponding 2024 and
  2020 polygons overlap by at least 99.95% of area in either direction,
  rounded to two decimal places (EPSG:5070). The 2026 links use names
  and the geographic comparison covers 2020 and 2024.
  All 184 contest totals reconcile within the official precinct report.
  The separate scanned countywide report could not be read for comparison.
