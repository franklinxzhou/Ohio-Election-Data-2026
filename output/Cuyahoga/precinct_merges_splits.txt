Crosswalk method
  County polygons were grouped by precinct name and intersected with
  the 2020 districts in equal-area EPSG:5070. A historical district
  is listed when it covers at least 2% of the current precinct.
  Each share describes current precinct area. The same-name match
  applies when it is the sole retained historical district.
  A changed label is recorded as renamed when the two footprints
  overlap by at least 98% in both directions, or split_precinct when
  the old district covers at least 98% of the current precinct and
  the current precinct covers less than 98% of the old district.
  The other changed links use boundary_overlap.
  Areas are geographic only. Each current precinct keeps a single
  vote row with its original totals.

  The GIS code BEDFD06B resolves the layer's duplicate BEDFORD-06-A
  label to BEDFORD-06-B. The unnamed OLMST00B subfeature is included
  with OLMSTED TOWNSHIP-00-B by GIS code. Census NORTH OLMSTEAD-03-B
  corresponds to graph label NORTH OLMSTED-03-B.
